Staff / admin screens · Slate (clean desktop, no glass)

The staff interface shares the palette and type with the public catalogue, but is a flat, high-legibility desktop admin — counters, not phones. Glass and full-bleed photos are deliberately absent: data entry needs contrast and speed (the ~400-game entry workflow). Brand green is used sparingly for primary actions and the Featured marker. Three core screens below: login, the game dashboard, and the add/edit form.
